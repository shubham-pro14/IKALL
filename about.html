WHO WE ARE

FINDING INSPIRATION IN EVERY TURN

I KALL is an electronic manufacturing company founded in 2015 by Mr. Ritesh Mehta that specializes in smartphones, tablets,
feature phones, smartwatches, and multimedia speakers. The company is dedicated to delivering innovative products that combine
high-quality user experiences with operational efficiency at honest prices, aiming to enhance lives through technology. With
six home furnishing units across Delhi and Noida and manufacturing facilities in Noida and Himachal Pradesh, I KALL has established
a significant market presence. By the end of 2020, it had expanded its reach to over 500 distributors, more than 25,000 retail
outlets, upwards of 2,000 service centers, and developed a portfolio of over 100 products, reflecting Mr. Mehta’s goal of
establishing his name as one of the prominent leaders in the electronics business.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities and dedicated workforce reflect our commitment to quality and innovation. Every device
we produce is built with precision and care, ensuring it meets the everyday needs of people from all walks of life—whether you’re
a student, a professional, or someone simply staying connected.

Our mission is to bridge the digital divide and empower users with products that are not only feature-rich but also easy to use and
budget-friendly. We find inspiration in every turn—every user, every experience, every challenge—and channel that into the
technology we deliver. From concept to creation, our devices are built with a focus on performance, durability, and affordability.
We’re proud to support local manufacturing and provide opportunities that fuel progress—both in technology and in
community development.
